CASPER FILES REQUIRED

You must extract vmlinuz and initrd from the Ubuntu 22.04.5 Live Server ISO.

Quick Method (Torrent - Fastest):
1. Download: https://releases.ubuntu.com/jammy/ubuntu-22.04.5-live-server-amd64.iso.torrent
2. After download, extract casper files:
   bsdtar -xf ubuntu-22.04.5-live-server-amd64.iso casper/vmlinuz casper/initrd
   mv casper/* ubuntu-22.04/casper/
   
Expected file sizes:
- vmlinuz: ~14 MB
- initrd:  ~95 MB

See ubuntu-22.04/casper/README.md for detailed instructions.
